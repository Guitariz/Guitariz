Guitar modes have a reputation for being confusing. Many guitarists are told: *"Dorian is just the C major scale starting on D."* While technically true in terms of pitch collection, that explanation fails to teach you how modes actually **sound** and how to use them in songwriting and guitar solos.

Modes are simply **moods**. They are variations of the standard Major and Minor scales created by altering a single note.

In this guide, we break down all 7 modes from brightest to darkest with clear formulas and iconic song examples.

---

## The 7 Modes Ranked from Brightest to Darkest

| Mode | Type | Defining Characteristic Note | Musical Mood | Famous Example Song |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Lydian** | Major | Raised 4th (`#4`) | Dreamy, sci-fi, celestial | *The Simpsons* Theme, *Flying in a Blue Dream* |
| **2. Ionian** | Major | Pure Natural Major (`1 2 3 4 5 6 7`) | Happy, bright, triumphant | *Let It Be*, *Twinkle Twinkle* |
| **3. Mixolydian** | Major | Flat 7th (`♭7`) | Bluesy, classic rock swagger | *Sweet Home Alabama*, *Norwegian Wood* |
| **4. Dorian** | Minor | Raised 6th (`♮6`) | Jazzy, sophisticated, uplifting minor | *So What* (Miles Davis), *Oye Como Va* |
| **5. Aeolian** | Minor | Natural Minor (`1 2 ♭3 4 5 ♭6 ♭7`) | Sad, emotional, epic | *Stairway to Heaven*, *Losing My Religion* |
| **6. Phrygian** | Minor | Flat 2nd (`♭2`) | Spanish, exotic, dark, tense | *White Rabbit* (Jefferson Airplane), Metal riffs |
| **7. Locrian** | Diminished | Flat 5th (`♭5`) & Flat 2nd (`♭2`) | Unstable, dissonant, eerie | Heavy metal, horror scores |

## Major Family vs. Minor Family

To use modes musically, think of them as modifications of either the standard **Major Scale (Ionian)** or **Minor Scale (Aeolian)**:

### The Major Modes
- **Ionian**: `1 – 2 – 3 – 4 – 5 – 6 – 7` (The baseline Major scale)
- **Lydian**: `1 – 2 – 3 – #4 – 5 – 6 – 7` (Major with `#4`)
- **Mixolydian**: `1 – 2 – 3 – 4 – 5 – 6 – ♭7` (Major with `♭7`)

### The Minor Modes
- **Aeolian**: `1 – 2 – ♭3 – 4 – 5 – ♭6 – ♭7` (The baseline Natural Minor scale)
- **Dorian**: `1 – 2 – ♭3 – 4 – 5 – 6 – ♭7` (Minor with natural `6`)
- **Phrygian**: `1 – ♭2 – ♭3 – 4 – 5 – ♭6 – ♭7` (Minor with `♭2`)
- **Locrian**: `1 – ♭2 – ♭3 – 4 – ♭5 – ♭6 – ♭7` (Minor with `♭5` and `♭2`)
