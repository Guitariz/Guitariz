Transcribing songs by ear is the ultimate musical superpower. When you can listen to a recording and immediately figure out the chord changes, melody, and rhythm, you become independent of unreliable online tab sites and sheet music.

In this guide, we walk through the exact 5-step framework that professional session musicians and transcribers use to dissect any song.

---

## Step 1: Find the Bass Notes First (The Golden Rule)

The secret of chord transcription is that **the bass note almost always defines the chord root**.

In 80% to 90% of popular music, the lowest sounding note (played by the bass guitar, kick-synth, or left hand on piano) is the root note of the underlying chord.

1. Listen strictly to the bassline. Hum or sing along with the lowest notes.
2. Find those single notes on your low E and A guitar strings.
3. If the bass plays `C → A → F → G`, your chord progression is almost certainly a variation of `C → Am → F → G`.

---

## Step 2: Determine Chord Quality (Major vs. Minor)

Once you have the root note, determine whether the chord is **Major or Minor**:

- **Test the 3rd**: Play the Major 3rd and Minor 3rd over the recording. One will clash heavily with the melody and harmony, while the other will lock in cleanly.
- **Check for Extensions**: Does the chord have a bluesy grit (Dominant 7th) or a lush, dreamy sheen (Major 7th or add9)? Use the **[Guitar Chord Library](/chords)** to compare voicings against the recording.

---

## Step 3: Identify Key and Form

Determine the home tonic (the "home base" chord that feels completely resolved).
- If the track resolves to a C major chord, you are likely in the key of C Major.
- Map out the song structure: Verse, Pre-Chorus, Chorus, Bridge, Outro.

Use the **[Guitariz Key Detector](/key-detector)** to verify the scale and tonal center in real time.

## Step 4: Isolate Difficult Sections with Audio Separation

When a mix is dense with layered synthesizers, backing vocals, and heavy drums, hearing individual guitar parts or subtle jazz chords can be difficult.

- Use the **[AI Stem Separator](/stem-separator)** to remove drums and vocals, leaving only pristine guitar, keyboard, and bass stems.
- Slow down tricky licks using speed controls to capture fast runs note by note.
