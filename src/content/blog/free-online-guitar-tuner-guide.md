## Standard Guitar Tuning (E A D G B E)

Standard 6-string guitar tuning arranges strings from lowest pitch (thickest string) to highest pitch (thinnest string):

| String # | Note | Scientific Pitch | Frequency (Hz) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **6 (Thickest)** | **E** | E2 | 82.41 Hz |
| **5** | **A** | A2 | 110.00 Hz |
| **4** | **D** | D3 | 146.83 Hz |
| **3** | **G** | G3 | 196.00 Hz |
| **2** | **B** | B3 | 246.94 Hz |
| **1 (Thinnest)** | **E** | E4 | 329.63 Hz |

An easy mnemonic to remember the string order is:
> **E**very **A**dult **D**og **G**rows **B**ig **E**ars
> or **E**ddie **A**te **D**ynamite **G**ood **B**ye **E**ddie!

## How to Use the Guitariz Chromatic Tuner

The **[Guitariz Tuner](/tuner)** utilizes the Web Audio API and autocorrelation pitch-detection algorithms directly on your computer, tablet, or smartphone microphone.

1. **Allow Microphone Access**: Click "Start Tuner" on the **[Online Tuner page](/tuner)**.
2. **Pluck a Single String**: Let the string ring out cleanly without muting other strings.
3. **Check the Cent Gauge**:
   - **Needle to the left (Negative Cents)**: The string is **flat** (too low) → Turn the tuning peg counter-clockwise to tighten and raise the pitch.
   - **Needle to the right (Positive Cents)**: The string is **sharp** (too high) → Turn the tuning peg clockwise to loosen and lower the pitch.
   - **Needle centered with green indicator**: The string is **in tune** (within ±1 cent).
4. **Repeat for all 6 strings**: Always tune from the low 6th string up to the 1st string.

## Pro Tuning Tips for Perfect Stability

- **Always Tune Up to the Pitch**: If a string is sharp, tune it *below* the target pitch first, then slowly tighten it up into tune. This prevents slack in the gear mechanism from causing slipping.
- **Stretch New Strings**: Fresh strings lose tension quickly. Gently pull them upward along the fretboard after installing and retune 2–3 times.
- **Check Octave Intonation**: Compare the open 12th fret harmonic to the fretted 12th note. If they differ, adjust your bridge saddle length.
