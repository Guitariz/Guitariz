## Why Musicians Are Leaving Chordify

Chordify launched in 2012 and was genuinely revolutionary at the time — the first widely available tool that could automatically synchronize chord labels with audio in a browser. For years, it remained largely free.

But the business model has evolved significantly:

- **Transposition is paywalled.** In the free tier, you cannot change the key of a song. A guitarist using a capo cannot quickly transpose a song to match their capo position without a paid subscription.
- **MIDI export is paywalled.** Producers who want to import chord progressions into a DAW must pay to unlock MIDI download.
- **No vocal isolation.** Chordify does not separate vocals from the mix before analysis, which significantly limits accuracy on pop and R&B tracks where the lead melody can interfere with chord detection.
- **Ad overload on free tier.** Multiple large banner and video ads appear on the chord view, often interrupting playback and making the interface cluttered and stressful to practice with.
- **Limited chord complexity.** Some more complex voicings (slash chords, extended chords) are less accurately represented in the free tier.

For a student, hobbyist, or independent musician who just needs to quickly learn a song, these restrictions add up to a frustrating experience.

## What to Look for in a Free Chordify Alternative

Before comparing platforms, here are the non-negotiable features modern musicians need from an AI chord transcribing tool:

1. **Unrestricted Chord Complexity:** Full support for 7th chords, minor 7ths, diminished, augmented, and slash chords (e.g., `C/E`, `G/B`). These voicings are standard in jazz, R&B, and contemporary pop.
2. **Instant Key Transposition:** Pitch-shift audio up or down in real time to match your capo position or vocal range. This should be free.
3. **MIDI Export:** Instant 1-click export of detected harmonic progressions to `.mid` files for DAW import. This is essential for any producer workflow.
4. **Vocal & Instrumental Stem Filtering:** AI-driven source separation to remove vocals before chord analysis on dense mixes — dramatically improves accuracy.
5. **No Registration or Paywall:** Instant browser analysis without forced account creation.
6. **Clean Interface:** An ad-free or minimal-ad experience that does not interrupt practice flow.

## 5. YouChords / Chords.ai (Browser Extensions)

Several browser extensions claim to detect chords from YouTube in real-time.

### The Catch:
Browser extension-based solutions are generally much less accurate than dedicated AI models because they process audio that has already been highly compressed by YouTube's streaming codec. They also often depend on web scraping of existing chord databases rather than true AI analysis of the audio content.

## Tips for Getting the Best Chord Detection Accuracy

Regardless of which tool you use, here are techniques that maximize accuracy:

**Use high-quality source audio.** A 320kbps MP3 or lossless WAV will produce significantly better chord detection than a 128kbps compressed file. Streaming-ripped audio often has codec artifacts that confuse pitch analysis.

**Pre-process with vocal removal on pop tracks.** Lead vocals occupy the same harmonic space as chord-defining notes and can cause misidentifications. Removing the vocal layer before analysis consistently improves accuracy, especially on R&B and modern pop.

**Cross-reference with the detected key.** Once you know the song's key, apply diatonic filtering: only 7 chords naturally occur in any given key. If the algorithm detects a chord that is "outside the key," it is likely either a wrong detection or a deliberate chromatic chord substitution worth verifying.

**Adjust the simplicity level.** If you need to play along immediately, enable Simple Chords mode to strip 7th and 9th extensions and get clean triads you can play on guitar right away.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Does Guitariz detect chords from YouTube videos?**

A: You can download the audio from a YouTube video (using any YouTube-to-MP3 converter) and upload the resulting MP3 file to Guitariz Chord AI for analysis.

**Q: Is there a song length limit on Guitariz?**

A: No. Unlike Moises's 5-minute free-tier cap, Guitariz has no song length restrictions.

**Q: How does AI chord detection compare to manual transcription by ear?**

A: AI chord detection is significantly faster and handles a wider range of chord types than most beginning-to-intermediate musicians can identify by ear. However, AI detection is not infallible — it can misidentify chords in very dense mixes, songs with ambiguous tonal centers, or recordings with extreme amounts of distortion or noise. A good workflow combines AI detection as a first pass with manual verification by ear for sections that sound wrong.

**Q: Can I use Guitariz to detect chords from live recordings?**

A: Yes. Upload any audio file — studio recording, live bootleg, or phone recording — and the chord detection engine will analyze it. Quality naturally degrades with lower recording quality, but usable chord outlines are often extractable even from phone recordings.
