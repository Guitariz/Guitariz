What is Guitariz Studio?

Guitariz Studio is an open-source, browser-based audio workstation and music education platform built by Abhinav Vaidya. Designed for guitarists, songwriters, producers, music educators, and audio engineers, Guitariz provides professional-grade digital signal processing (DSP) and artificial intelligence tools completely free of charge, with no subscriptions, account walls, or artificial usage caps.

Every tool runs with a hybrid processing architecture: client-side Web Audio API algorithms execute privately inside your browser for zero-latency feedback, while compute-intensive tasks (such as deep learning source separation) are processed via secure, stateless backend pipelines.

Core Studio Features & Tools

1. Chord AI & Harmonic Recognition

Our intelligent chord recognition engine transcribes audio recordings into detailed chord charts and synchronized interactive fretboard fingerings. The engine utilizes Harmonic-Percussive Source Separation (HPSS) to filter out transient drum noise, extracts 12-dimensional chromagram features, and applies Viterbi-based Hidden Markov Models (HMM) in log-space to ensure musically coherent transitions between major, minor, dominant 7th, and extended chord voicings.

2. AI Stem Separator & Vocal Remover

Powered by Meta AI's state-of-the-art Demucs v4 (HTDemucs) deep neural network, our audio separator allows musicians and producers to isolate up to 6 distinct audio tracks: vocals, drums, bass, electric guitar, piano, and other accompaniment. Generate crystal-clear acapellas, backing tracks for karaoke, or isolate instrument stems for detailed musical transcription.

3. Scale Explorer & Indian Classical Raga Bridge

Visualize over 100 scales and modes across a full 24-fret interactive guitar fretboard and 88-key piano keyboard. In addition to standard Western modes (Ionian, Dorian, Phrygian, Lydian, Mixolydian, Aeolian, Locrian) and pentatonic/blues scales, Guitariz uniquely features Indian Classical Raga theory (including Bhairav, Yaman, Bilawal, Kafi, Asavari, Bhairavi, Darbari, and Malkauns) mapped directly onto standard guitar fretboard coordinates.

4. Circle of Fifths & Music Theory Wheel

An interactive Circle of Fifths wheel designed for songwriting, harmonic analysis, and rapid key transposition. Instantly view the 7 diatonic chords for any key signature, identify relative minor/major pairs, and construct balanced chord progressions based on functional harmony principles.

5. BPM & Key Detection Suite

Analyze rhythmic tempo (Beats Per Minute) and tonal key signatures directly from local MP3, WAV, and audio files. Features automated spectral onset envelope detection, half-time/double-time toggles, an interactive Tap Tempo calculator, and Krumhansl-Kessler chromagram correlation for Camelot-wheel harmonic DJ mixing.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is Guitariz Studio completely free to use?

Yes. All core tools—including AI Chord Recognition, Stem Separation, Scale Explorer, Fretboard, Tuner, Metronome, and Music Theory labs—are 100% free with no subscription or mandatory registration required.

Are my uploaded audio files kept private and secure?

Yes. We prioritize user privacy. Client-side tools (like the tuner, fretboard, and metronome) process audio strictly in your local browser. Audio uploaded for stem separation or chord AI is processed securely and is never stored permanently on our servers.
